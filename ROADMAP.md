# Roadmap

Where Tarkib is going. These are plans, not promises, and there are no dates.

## Planned

- **Preview before adding.** See the finished card first, edit any text, then add it or throw it
  away. Off by default, so the one-click path stays one click.
- **Regenerate one part.** A button to rewrite a single sentence, the translation or the emojis,
  with an optional hint such as "more formal" or "about cooking".
- **Next photo.** Swap the picture for the next search result, without a new AI call.
- **Batch mode.** Paste a list of words and review the cards one by one: keep, regenerate or skip.
- **"Don't confuse with".** An optional line for word pairs learners mix up, such as legen and
  liegen, or kennen and wissen.

## Principles

Every new feature has to keep these. A feature that breaks one is the wrong feature.

1. **One click stays enough.** Type a word, click Add, done. Previews and edits are optional.
2. **The card is a normal Anki note.** It studies the same on AnkiDroid and iPhone, with no add-on
   installed there.
3. **No extra automatic AI calls per card.** Free plans have tight limits. New work goes into the
   main call or behind a button the user clicks.
4. **The content rules always apply,** also to anything regenerated: no alcohol or pork in
   examples, the preposition with its case, German that fits the level, Modern Standard Arabic,
   safe images. The full list is in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
5. **A failed step never breaks the card.** If something optional fails, the card is still added.

## Not planned

- A paid version of the core. Making good cards stays free.
- Features that only work inside the add-on and would break the card on other devices.

## Ideas

Missing something? Open a [discussion](https://github.com/saadel123/tarkib/discussions) or an
[issue](https://github.com/saadel123/tarkib/issues). Help with more languages and native-speaker
tuning of the prompts is the most welcome of all.
